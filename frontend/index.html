Find classical sources that may anticipate a herbal-medicine patent

Enter a plant, a condition, or the substance of a patent claim. The tool searches the Charaka Samhita for passages that name the same materia medica and indications, and separately searches Indian patent filings in IPC class A61K 36.

Plants and conditions are recognised in English, botanical Latin and romanised Sanskrit (ITRANS or IAST), so turmeric, Curcuma longa and haridrā retrieve the same passages.

Where no passage meets the evidence threshold, the tool says so instead of returning the nearest guess. Results are research leads for a qualified examiner, not legal opinions.
